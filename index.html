How to download the game files

  1. Go to github.com/arissafram/camp-ko-games in your browser.
  2. Click the green Code button near the top right.
  3. Click Download ZIP. A file called camp-ko-games-main.zip will download.
  4. Find the ZIP in your Downloads folder. Double-click it to unzip.
  5. Open the templates/ folder. Pick a game. Open starter/index.html in your browser to start.
