Make It Yours

  • 🎨 Design your own look with colors, images, fonts, and sounds.
  • 🎮 Make it a single-player game, a two-player battle, or even a six-player competition.
  • 🖱️ Decide how players score—mouse clicks, keyboard presses, or something completely different.
  • ⏱️ Add a timer to see who can score the most before time runs out.
  • 🏆 Save the highest score and challenge your friends.
  • 🎯 Replace the button with moving targets, bouncing objects, or anything else you can imagine.
