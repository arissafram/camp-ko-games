🔮 Magic 8 Ball

What are you building?

A game where someone asks a question and gets a randomly chosen response.

Make it yours

It doesn’t have to be a Magic 8 Ball. Turn it into a fortune teller, advice machine, mysterious oracle, terrible life coach, compliment generator, or something completely different.

Success looks like

  • The player can ask a question
  • The game chooses a random response
  • Your version feels like your own

Want to go further?

  • Write your own responses
  • Change the message when no question is entered
  • Add different types of answers
  • Add an animation or sound
  • Stretch: Make certain questions trigger special responses

Helpful Tricks

Pick something randomly

First, choose a random spot in an array:

let randomIndex = Math.floor(Math.random() * answers.length);

Then grab the answer in that spot:

let answer = answers[randomIndex];

Read what someone typed

let question = questionInput.value;

Change text on the page

answerDisplay.textContent = answer;
